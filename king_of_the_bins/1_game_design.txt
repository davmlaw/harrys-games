King of the Bins — Harry's design (November 2026, as told to Claude)

Okay, so we want to make a new game that is basically a side-scroller, like sort of Mario style, except the main
character is a raccoon. And it's called King of the Bins. And basically you jump around a suburban neighborhood full
of old-style metal bins in a dark night, suburbs and stuff. And you run around knocking over bins. So I guess if you
run into a bin, it knocks it over, like, I guess, a physics simulator style with the lids flying off and all the
rubbish being strewn everywhere. And you can run around and then rummage through the rubbish bins and trash bins and
get treasure or food and power-ups and stuff. And then humans will try and catch you. And if they'll put you in a cage
and take you away, and that's how you lose the game.

So it should look — the title screen should have a picture of a raccoon's face on it, and he kind of looks like a
baddie, like he looks maybe he should be put in like a prison. Like he looks like a bandit, because raccoons are
bandits. At the end he gets put in like white stripes: you see the raccoon's face and then you see him get suddenly
put in the old style white stripe cartoon prisoner's outfit, and then slammed bars appear on him, and that's the end
you get when you get caught.
